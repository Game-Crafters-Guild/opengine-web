@openengine/web examples

A WebGPU renderer and an entity-component world behind a small JavaScript API. Each example is one page: its engine calls in main.js, its controls in a script beside it. They need a browser with WebGPU: current Chrome or Edge.

  • Model viewer: pick a sample model or open your own .glb, orbit it, set the time of day, stop or start the turntable, and show the frame rate. 55 lines of engine calls in main.js; 273 lines of page and panel. Models: Khronos glTF Sample Assets, CC0 1.0, CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-NC 4.0. Early access (alpha).
  • Instancing: one model drawn up to 10,000 times on a spiral, every copy an entity turning about world up each frame; set the count with a slider and show the frame rate. 59 lines of engine calls in main.js; 119 lines of page and panel. Model: Avocado, Khronos glTF Sample Assets, CC0 1.0. Early access (alpha).
  • Skinning: an animated fox; pick one of its clips, set the playback speed, pause and resume. 46 lines of engine calls in main.js; 128 lines of page and panel. Model: Fox, Khronos glTF Sample Assets, CC0 1.0 (mesh) and CC BY 4.0 (rigging, animation and conversion), credited on the page. Early access (alpha).

The pages share 90 lines of status, download bar and frame-rate code in examples/shared/.

Every asset's source and license is listed in ASSET_PROVENANCE.md. The pages load coi-serviceworker.js (MIT) to run the threaded build on hosts that cannot send isolation headers.
